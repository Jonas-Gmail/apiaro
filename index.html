Apiaro

A record book for beekeepers: apiaries, colonies, inspections, varroa, treatments, queens and honey.

Support

Questions, feedback or a problem with the app? Write to jonas.volk89@gmail.com – we usually answer within two working days.

To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchases". Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

Moving to a new iPhone: your data is included in your iCloud or computer backup. You can also create a backup file in the app (Menu → Records & labels → Export backup) and restore it on the new iPhone.

Notice

Apiaro calculates and stores what you enter. It does not recommend treatments, products or doses. Always follow the product information and your vet, bee inspector or bee health service. Rules for records, registration and withdrawal periods differ from country to country; please check which ones apply to you.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Apiaro ("the app") is developed by Jonas Volk, Switzerland. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the app.

No data collection

Apiaro does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter – apiaries, colonies, inspections, varroa counts, treatments, feedings, harvests, queens, tasks, your name and registration number – is stored only on your device. If you use iCloud device backups, this data may be included in your backup under Apple's privacy policy. You can delete all data in the app (Settings → Delete all data); deleting the app also deletes all of its data.

Camera

If you allow it, the camera is used only to read the QR labels on your hives. Images are processed live on your device and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Microphone and dictation

If you use dictation during an inspection, Apiaro uses Apple's speech recognition in on-device mode only. The audio is turned into text on your iPhone, is not recorded and is not sent to us or to Apple. If your iPhone can't recognise your language offline, dictation is simply not available.

PDF records and backups

PDFs, QR label sheets and backup files are created on your device. They only leave your device if you choose to share or save them somewhere, and then only to the place or recipient you pick.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, task and queen rearing reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information. Apple shares only anonymous, aggregated sales information with developers.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
